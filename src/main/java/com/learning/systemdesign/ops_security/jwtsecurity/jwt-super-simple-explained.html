🔒 Why Can't Someone Fake It?
🎨
Think of a $100 bill:

Anyone can LOOK at a $100 bill.
Anyone can PHOTOCOPY it.

BUT!

Real money has a special watermark 💧
Only the government knows how to make it!

If you try to print fake money → No watermark → Fake! 🚫
JWT has a "digital watermark" called SIGNATURE
The Secret Ink Story:

1️⃣ Website has special invisible ink (Secret Key)
→ Only the website knows the ink formula!

2️⃣ When making your wristband, it uses this ink
→ Creates the signature: "xyz123"

3️⃣ Bad guy tries to change wristband:
→ Changes "john" to "admin"

4️⃣ But bad guy doesn't have the special ink!
→ Can't make a valid signature for "admin"

5️⃣ Website checks signature:
→ "This signature doesn't match! FAKE!" 🚫

⚠️ Important Things to Know
1. Anyone Can READ Your Wristband!

The wristband is NOT a secret! 🚫
It's like a name tag → Anyone can see your name

NEVER put your password in JWT!
NEVER put credit card in JWT!

Only put: Your username, maybe your role (admin/user)
2. Wristband Expires!

Just like a concert wristband is only good for ONE day 📅
JWT expires after some time (usually 15 minutes to 1 day)

After expiration → You need to log in again! 🔄
3. Keep Your Wristband Safe!

If someone steals your wristband → They can pretend to be you! 😱

That's why websites use HTTPS (the padlock 🔒 in browser)
Encrypts everything so hackers can't steal your wristband!
